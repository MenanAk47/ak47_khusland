**The ultimate cannabis dispensary, lounge, and nightlife experience for FiveM servers.** ak47_khusland completely revamps dispensary and lounge roleplay with an all-in-one business featuring seed-to-shelf weed farming, interactive hookah tables, dynamic cocktail mixing, and synchronized pole dancing. Players interact through a sleek tablet NUI supporting customer purchasing, recipe crafting, and live inventory price management, fully powered by `ak47_lib` for seamless multi-framework compatibility.

## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 🌿 Seed-to-Harvest Cannabis Farming

* **Growth Stages & Maintenance:** Plant seeds into pots, monitor real-time growth stages, and maintain plant health with required fertilizer and pest sprays to prevent damage.
* **Harvesting & Processing:** Harvest mature weed leaves and process them into packaged cannabis, joints, backwoods, and custom concentrates via workstation tables.

### 📱 Modern Tablet POS, Workstation & Management

* **Customer Storefront:** Intuitive digital catalog featuring category filters (Flavours, Backwoods, Joints, Vapes, Utilities), search functionality, shopping cart, and cash or card checkout.
* **Workstation Crafting:** In-tablet recipe workstation allowing employees to craft edibles, pre-rolls, and specialty items with clear ingredient requirements.
* **Management & Stock Console:** Authorized managers and owners can deposit and withdraw shop inventory in real time and modify unit selling prices on the fly.

### 💨 Interactive Hookah Lounge & Custom Edibles

* **Hookah Stations with Synced Smoke:** Interactive shisha tables with physical hose attachment to the ped, puff limits, realistic inhale/exhale sound effects, and persistent table refill mechanics.
* **Custom 3D Edible Props:** Includes custom 3D props (Blaze Bites, Chronic Crunch Cookies, Herbal Delight Gummies, High Time Brownies, Kush Krisps) with matching plates, eating animations, and hunger, thirst, and stress relief.

### 🍸 Bar Crafting, Stage Entertainment & Tipping

* **Realistic Bar Preparation:** Bartenders can prepare cocktails and pour drinks using authentic cocktail shaker animations, props, and refillable drink dispensers.
* **Synchronized Pole Dancing:** Synchronized stage pole dancing with multiple switchable animations and an interactive spectator tipping prompt.
* **Bartender Tipping Zone:** Designated bar tip zone allowing patrons to reward working bartenders directly via cash or card.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Complete Business Economy:** Built-in society fund integration for business purchasing, 6 job grades, JSON datastore persistence for plants and bar stock, and optional auto-stock regeneration.

* Download ak47_khusland from your cfx portal.
* Add the script in your resources folder.
* Add items based on your framework instruction.
* Add item images into inventory.
* Start the script in your server.cfg below framework files.
* Make sure you have all dependencies.
* Restart the server.
